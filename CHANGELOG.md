# Changelog

## 0.1.0

First public release.

- Scan npm, pnpm, Yarn and Bun lockfiles, and PyPI, crates.io, Go, RubyGems and Packagist lockfiles, with the hosted scanner at security.togoder.click
- `estimate` shows what needs scanning, and the price, before you pay
- Text report, JSON and SARIF 2.1.0 output; exit codes for CI
- Stable finding IDs and an `.npm-scanner-ignore` list
- `login`, `logout`, `account` and `config` commands
